03 — About

I ship end to end.

The API, the data model, the third-party integrations and the interface people use all day — one developer, the whole thing.

Most of my work sits where software meets money and logistics. On TAS Portals that means airline distribution — search, pricing, hold and ticketing over GDS and IATA NDC — backed by agency wallets and a double-entry ledger. On Kashpoint it means a cashier screen that has to be fast on a Saturday afternoon, inventory that stays in sync across branches, and subscription billing that just works.

I care about the unglamorous parts: money that balances, migrations that don't break a release, and APIs that are easy to reason about.
